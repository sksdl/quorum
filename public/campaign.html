How the payment works

  1. PayPal places a hold for the total. Nothing is charged today.
  2. When pre-orders close, each item is made only if it reached its factory minimum (or the creator decides to make it anyway).
  3. You are charged for the items that are made, plus shipping once. The rest of the hold is released, so there is no refund to wait for. (If you paid by card, your card issuer may show the released hold as pending for a few days.)
