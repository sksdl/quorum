Pre-orders that only charge for what gets made.

Small-batch makers have to promise a factory minimum before they know demand. Quorum takes pre-orders as PayPal authorizations, then captures only the items that go to the factory. The rest of each hold is released, so there is no refund to process.

Paste your post

Write the pre-order post and paste the factory quote the way you already do, in Korean or any language. AI turns them into a campaign with prices, price breaks and shipping, and asks about anything unclear.

Fans place a hold

Fans abroad pay with PayPal. With intent=AUTHORIZE, PayPal holds the amount and charges nothing yet. Progress toward each item's minimum is public.

Decide per item

At close, Quorum computes cost, profit and leftover stock for every option. AI recommends go or cancel using your own notes. You approve.

Charge exactly that

Partial capture for each fan, voids where nothing is made, a note in their language on the PayPal receipt, and tracking numbers added to each capture.

Who it is for

  • Illustrators selling goods of their own characters (acrylic keyrings, stands, stickers, pins) to overseas fans, who today run a non-binding demand survey on a form and then chase payments.
  • Zine makers, keycap designers, ceramic studios: anyone whose factory or print shop has a minimum order and price breaks.
