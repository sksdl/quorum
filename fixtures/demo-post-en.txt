[Overseas mail order] Studio Haneul <Night Market> goods: taking pre-orders

Many fans abroad asked during the last demand survey, so this time I'm taking pre-orders for overseas shipping separately.

Items that don't reach their minimum aren't made, and that amount is released, not charged.

Items (overseas prices in dollars, shipping extra)
1. Acrylic keyring, double-sided, 6 cm - Korea 12,000 won / overseas 9 dollars
2. Acrylic stand, 12 cm (base included) - Korea 22,000 won / overseas 16 dollars
3. Holographic sticker, A6 - Korea 6,000 won / overseas 5 dollars
4. Enamel pin, 3 cm, gold plated - Korea 19,000 won / overseas 14 dollars

Pre-orders are open for ten days.
After the deadline I send the order to the factory, and everything ships together at the end of November by Korea Post K-Packet.
Shipping: Asia 5 dollars, Americas and Oceania 7 dollars, Europe and everywhere else 8 dollars

My studio is small, so I can keep only about 10 leftover units per item.
Stickers and keyrings can be sold at events, so a few leftovers are fine.
The pin needs a separate mold fee, so if the count is borderline I'll skip it this time.
